Under the hood · honest stack

What we run, why, and what it costs.

Vision + reasoning

Claude Opus 4.7 (1M ctx) — for brand-aware decisions, voice writing, grid planning. Heavy prompt-caching cuts cost ~80%. Used sparingly — once per event, not per image.

Florence-2 — cheap auto-labeling per image (subject category, composition signal). $0.0002/image.

Visual ops

SAM 3 (Replicate) — subject segmentation. ~95% mask quality on portraits, 80% on crowds. Latency ~2s. $0.0035/image.

Flux Fill — outpainting fallback when SAM mask fails. Same provider, $0.011/image. Used <10% of the time.

Embeddings + memory

SigLIP-2 (768-dim) — image embeddings for "find more like this" + brand similarity. Stored in Supabase pgvector.

Captions + voice

Claude Haiku — bulk caption generation. $0.001/caption. Brand voice doc passed in cached prefix; each new event gets consistent tone for free.

Honest about the risks

Three things that could kill us — and how we plan to handle each.

Meta Graph API access

Auto-publishing to Instagram requires Meta to approve our app for IG Business. Approval takes 4–8 weeks. Mitigation: ship Mode B (semi-manual) on day one — system opens IG with photo + caption preloaded, the photographer taps Publish. ~7 seconds per post. Auto-publish ships when Meta approves.

"I trust Yael more than AI"

The agency owner objection. Mitigation: nothing publishes without human approval. Promarket reviews everything. AI is the speed multiplier, not the decision maker. Position piXflow as the brief Yael wished she had — not Yael's replacement on day one.

Adobe ships this in Lightroom

Probability low — Adobe doesn't own the calendar. They monetize creator tools, not B2B social mgmt. Mitigation: if Adobe ships visual feed planning in 2026, they ship for individual creators. We're selling to production companies who buy on the social-mgmt SKU, not the editing SKU. Different buyer, different sales motion.

What we're not promising

We are not promising that the AI will handle strategy in v1 — campaign theming across a quarter, audience segmentation, response writing. v1 = visual + caption + grid + schedule. Strategy is v2.
