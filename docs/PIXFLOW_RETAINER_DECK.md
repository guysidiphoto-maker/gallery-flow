## The Problem

Production companies produce 2-4 events per week. After each event:

❌ Photos sit in a folder for weeks  
❌ Social media gets 2-3 random posts — months late  
❌ The website hasn't been updated since 2023  
❌ Every tender requires hours of digging through old photos  
❌ Vendors call asking "where are my photos?"  
❌ No one knows which content performs and which doesn't  

**The production company is losing business because their work is invisible.**

Pixflow turns the photographer into a **creative operations manager**.

After every event, the photographer uploads photos to Pixflow. From that moment, **everything happens automatically:**

| What | How | When |

| Content calendar for next 4 weeks is built | AI | Within minutes |
| Client can browse and approve content | Dashboard | Anytime |
| Monthly analytics report is generated | AI + Data | Monthly |
| Tender response with matching photos | AI | 2 minutes |

**The production company does nothing. Everything is ready.**

### 2. AI-Powered Social Media Content

The photographer selects Top Picks (one keystroke: T). From that moment:

#### Instagram Feed — Designed, Not Random

AI doesn't just pick photos. It **designs the feed**:

- **Color flow** — analyzes the color palette of each photo and arranges them so the Instagram grid has visual coherence. Blue event next to blue-toned photo, warm event next to warm tones.
- **Rhythm** — alternates between wide shots, close-ups, and detail shots for visual variety.
- **Recency weighting** — newer events get priority but old events aren't forgotten.

#### LinkedIn — Professional Tone

Same photos, completely different text:

> **Instagram:** "800 people. 3 stages. One unforgettable night ✨ #eventproduction #conference"
>
> **LinkedIn:** "We're proud to have produced the IAC 2026 Annual Conference in Tel Aviv. Over 800 industry leaders gathered for three days of innovation, networking, and inspiration. Thank you to our incredible team and partners for making this happen."

#### Content Calendar

AI doesn't just generate content — it **schedules it strategically**:

- **Monday:** Professional LinkedIn post (business hours engagement)
- **Wednesday:** Instagram carousel (mid-week scroll peak)
- **Friday:** Fun behind-the-scenes / Instagram Story (weekend mood)
- **Spacing:** Never two posts from the same event in a row
- **Anniversaries:** "1 year ago today, we produced [event]" auto-reminders

### 3. Pitch Deck Generator

Production company gets a tender? 2 minutes, not 2 hours.

**Step 1:** Paste the tender requirements  
**Step 2:** AI matches similar past events  
**Step 3:** AI selects the best photos  
**Step 4:** Download a designed PDF or share a link

The PDF is **designed** — not a PowerPoint with photos thrown in:
- Cover page with company branding
- Each event gets a spread with key stats
- Photos are laid out professionally
- Auto-generated text describes the company's experience
- Contact page at the end

### 4. Vendor Auto-Delivery

After each event, the photographer tags photos by vendor (catering, DJ, venue, etc.).

Each vendor gets a **unique link**:
```
pixflow-ai.com/vendor/X7K9M2
```

They see only their photos. They download what they need. **No emails, no WeTransfer, no "can you send me the photos?"**

**Why this matters for growth:** Every vendor who uses the link sees "Powered by Pixflow." They tell their photographer: "Why don't you use this?" → **Organic growth.**
